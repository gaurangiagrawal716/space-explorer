Famous space missions

People have been exploring space for more than 60 years. Here are some key moments.

Milestones

YearMissionWhat happened
1957Sputnik 1First artificial satellite orbits Earth.
1961Vostok 1Yuri Gagarin becomes the first human in space.
1969Apollo 11First humans walk on the Moon.
1977Voyager 1 and 2Probes launched to explore the outer planets.
1990Hubble TelescopeLaunched to take sharp pictures of deep space.
2021PerseveranceRover lands on Mars to search for signs of past life.

Why explore space?

  • To understand how the solar system formed.
  • To search for life beyond Earth.
  • To develop new technology, like satellites for GPS and weather.
