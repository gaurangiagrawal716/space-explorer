Milestones in space exploration

A timeline of the missions that changed what we know about space.

  1. 1957

    Sputnik 1

    The Soviet Union launches the first artificial satellite and begins the space age.

  2. 1961

    Yuri Gagarin

    The first human to travel to space completes one orbit of Earth.

  3. 1969

    Apollo 11

    Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin become the first people to walk on the Moon.

  4. 1977

    Voyager 1 and 2

    Twin probes tour the outer planets. Both later reach interstellar space.

  5. 1990

    Hubble Space Telescope

    Orbiting above the atmosphere, it sends back sharp images of distant galaxies.

  6. 1998

    International Space Station

    The first module launches. Crews from many countries have lived aboard ever since.

  7. 2012

    Curiosity rover

    NASA lands a car-sized rover on Mars to study whether the planet could once support life.

  8. 2021

    Perseverance and James Webb

    Perseverance lands on Mars with the Ingenuity helicopter, and the James Webb telescope launches to study the early universe.
